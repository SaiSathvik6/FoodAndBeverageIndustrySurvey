# 🥤 Market Insights Dashboard – CodeX Energy Drink Launch (India)

## 🧭 Overview
This project presents **data-driven insights** from a consumer survey conducted by **CodeX**, a German beverage company, to evaluate the early performance of their energy drink in the Indian market.

After launching the product in **10 cities**, the company gathered responses from **10,000 participants** to better understand:
- Customer awareness and perception
- Brand positioning against competitors
- Purchase behavior and preferences

As a Marketing Analyst, the goal was to **transform raw survey results into actionable insights** using an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
**CodeX** is entering a competitive Indian market and needs to assess:
- How well customers **recognize the brand**
- Which marketing channels are **most effective**
- What consumer preferences should drive **product development**
- How CodeX compares with **existing competitors**

The marketing team needs **visual, data-backed answers** to fine-tune strategy and increase market share.

---

## 🧰 Tools & Tech Stack
- **Power BI** – Dashboard design & KPI tracking
- **Power Query** – Data transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated columns & measures
- **Excel / CSV** – Survey data source

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- 🌐 **City-wise Brand Awareness Comparison**
- 📈 **Purchase Intent vs Competitor Brands**
- 💬 **Customer Feedback Themes**
- 🔍 **Demographic Breakdown** (Age, Gender, Income)
- 📢 **Marketing Channel Effectiveness**
- 📦 **Suggestions for Product/Flavor Preferences**
## 🧠 Insights Derived
- Cities like **Delhi** and **Bangalore** showed the highest **brand recall**
- **Taste** and **price point** were major factors in **repeat purchase intent**
- **Social media ads** and **influencer campaigns** drove the most engagement
- Consumers preferred **sugar-free** and **tropical flavor variants**
